For a salon offering occasion styling.

One recommendation · illustrative

Three wedding-guest hairstyles

Hook: “Wedding this weekend? Three looks you can save now.”

Storyboard: Show three finished looks, one close-up per style, and a final invitation to book. Film with a phone in good natural light.

Why this might fit: If styling tutorials performed well in the salon’s recent posts, another practical tutorial could be worth testing. Current trend evidence would need to be checked separately. This is a suggestion, not a performance guarantee.
